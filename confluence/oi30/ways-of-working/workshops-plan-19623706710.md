#### **Pre-requisite for the workshops **

SOW Signed off.

Infra sorted for the team - Azure

StatusNeo LT Team onboarding

Git, JIRA, Confluence, Figma, Miro ready.

Initial Functional & Non-Functional requirements documented.

#### **Kick-off Workshop objectives**

- Review already decided team alignment.
- Realign on project objectives, scope, success criteria, and governance.
- Review key stakeholders, roles, and responsibilities.
- Agree communication cadence, decision-making process, and project milestones.
- Identify and document key risks, dependencies, and assumptions.
- Participants - Bain LT, StatusNeo LT

#### **Business Kick-off Workshop (2–3 days)**

**Dates:** **4–5/6 August**

- Review business vision, objectives, and expected outcomes.
- Prioritise business requirements and use cases.
- Re-access MVP scope and delivery priorities.
- Agree business KPIs and measures of success.
- Identify process changes and operational impacts.

#### **Technical workshop objectives**

**Week commencing 10 August**

- Team onboarding and environment setup progress discussions.
- Review existing architecture, applications, and infrastructure.
- Walkthrough of current systems and integrations.
- Review security, networking, and platform standards.
- Confirm development approach, tooling, and ways of working.
- Identify technical risks, constraints, and dependencies.
- Participants - Bain Architects (Solution & Data), Bain’s tech lead, BCN Tech,, StatusNeo technical Leads.

#### **Architecture Workshops (2 days)**

- Review current-state architecture.
- Define target architecture and guiding principles.
- Discuss integration patterns and APIs.
- Review scalability, resilience, and security considerations.
- Agree architecture decisions and technical standards.
- Produce high-level solution architecture.

#### **Data & Application Design Workshops (2–3 days)**

- Define application boundaries and responsibilities.
- Review data model and data flows.
- Design integration interfaces and event flows.
- Discuss reporting and analytics requirements.
- Review non-functional requirements (performance, availability, security).
- Agree application design and implementation approach.
- Identify backlog items and design decisions.

#### **Project Progress & Direction Workshop**

**Week commencing 14 September (1 week)**

- Review project progress against milestones.
- Demonstrate completed work and validate outcomes.
- Assess technical and architectural decisions made to date.
- Resolve outstanding risks, issues, and blockers.
- Review lessons learned from the initial phase.
- Refine roadmap and delivery plan.
- Reassess priorities based on stakeholder feedback.
- Confirm future technical and architectural direction.
- Discuss redesign opportunities where required.
- Agree next-phase scope and implementation approach.
- Finalise action items, owners, and timelines.
- Immediately after MVP release - evaluate where we are and what do we need for scaling
