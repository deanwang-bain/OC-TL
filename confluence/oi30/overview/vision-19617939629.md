# Vision

[View in Confluence](https://bainco.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OI30/pages/19617939629)

**Product vision:**

OI 3.0 is an AI-powered tool designed to help Bain partners prepare for client conversations faster. When a partner wants have a first discussion with a client or prepare a proposal, the tool automates the research, benchmarking, and deck creation that today takes a team of COEs and consultants days to complete. The goal is to compress that entire process to roughly 30 minutes, and give the partner confidence walking into a CEO meeting.

**Design vision:**

OI 3.0 is designed to be non-linear and modular, so partners can jump back to adjust peers or context mid-analysis and only the affected modules rerun. The design also emphasizes transparency, partners can drill into any opportunity to see the data sources, confidence levels, and reasoning behind it.

Partners retain judgment; agents do synthesis. The tool uses hard gates (blocks until the partner resolves something critical, like uploading financials for a private company) and soft gates (proceeds but flags lower confidence). The philosophy is "AI as enabler, not decision-maker" — partners are accountable for what goes in front of clients.

**Longer-term vision:**

Beyond the MVP, OI 3.0 is envisioned as an ongoing portfolio tool — not just a one-time deck generator. For e.g., after a partner exports a deck for Nike, the tool continues monitoring Nike's peers for earnings releases, news, and strategic moves that might shift the narrative, surfacing updates in an overnight digest.
