# Design Principles

[View in Confluence](https://bainco.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OI30/pages/19628884086)

### **Partner Judgment, Not Agent Decisions**

The agent handles the heavy lifting (research, benchmarking, candidate generation) but every consequential call belongs to the partner. Judgment cards make this explicit: wherever the analysis reaches a decision point, the tool pauses and puts the decision in the partner's hands. The** partner is accountable **for what goes in front of a client, not the AI.

### **Radical Transparency, Not Black Boxes**

Trust is the central adoption barrier. Partners won't act on output they can't interrogate. Every number, recommendation, and opportunity **must be drillable** e.g., showing the data sources, confidence level, and reasoning behind it. If confidence is low, the partner can act on it by uploading more data, not just note it and move on.

### **A Thought Partner, Not a Calculator**

The agent shouldn't just return results, it should behave like a good consultant in the room. That means proactively flagging outliers, offering a hypothesis, and inviting a reaction. And when the partner pushes back via the persistent chat, at any point in the flow, the tool responds, adjusts, and reruns. The **interaction is a dialogue**, not a query.

### **Modular Flow, Not Linear Steps**

Partners don't work in straight lines. They change a peer mid-analysis, revisit context after seeing the numbers, or upload a document they forgot. The tool should let them and only rerun the modules that are affected. The** experience feels like a workspace **you move around in, not a wizard you complete.

### **Co-creation, Not Solo Work**

Sizing up an opportunity is never a one-person exercise. Partners co-create with other partners, align with APs, and loop in account leads. The **project should feel like a shared object from the start**. The experience must account for multiple people touching the same analysis at different times: who made a change, who holds the pen at any given moment. Handoffs should be seamless, not an afterthought.
