### Peer Set Analysis Methodology

### Peer Identification Process Optimization

Sharma explained the process for identifying comparable peers for a company, which involves providing a simple prompt to generate a ranked list of potential peers based on business factors, geography, revenue, and scale. The team currently uses a combination of CAP-IQ analysis and ChatGPT tools like Claude to optimize the peer suggestion process, but they still rely on CAP-IQ for checking reporting structures due to ChatGPT's limitations in handling larger peer sets and reading reports accurately.

### EBIT vs EBITDA Analysis Framework

Sharma explained that the basis for using EBIT or EBITDA depends on whether DNA is a separate line item in the client's income statement. If DNA is a separate line item, analysis is based at the EBITDA level, but if it's bucketed under COGS and SG&A without being a separate line item, analysis is based at the EBIT level. Sharma also mentioned that companies typically report both EBIT and EBITDA in their 10-K filings, with the primary analysis level determined by which metric appears first in the income statement.

### 10K Reports Analysis Overview

Sharma explained that 10K reports are the main source used to analyze company performance, including metrics like gross margin, SG&A, and revenue changes. Sharma described how 10Ks help identify reporting structures, required adjustments, non-recurring items, and M&A activities that might affect financial reporting periods. Sharma noted that for North American companies, non-GAAP results are often found in press releases rather than just in the 10K reports.

### Earnings Releases vs 10-Ks Explanation

Sharma explained the difference between earnings releases and 10-Ks, noting that while both contain similar information like income statements and balance sheets, earnings releases more commonly include non-GAAP and non-recurring information. Sharma clarified that when calculating medians and top quartiles, the target company is included alongside peers in these calculations. The discussion ended with Michelle asking about NWC formulas, but the transcript cuts off before Sharma's response.

### Cost Calculation Methods Across Industries

Sharma explained that different industries use varying approaches for cost calculations in formulas like DIO and DPO, with healthcare being the main exception that includes total costs rather than just COGS in the denominator. Sharma agreed to share industry-specific BDPs, formulas, and watch-outs with Michelle, including details about how ASOS computations remain unaffected by these cost calculation methods since they use revenue in the denominator.
