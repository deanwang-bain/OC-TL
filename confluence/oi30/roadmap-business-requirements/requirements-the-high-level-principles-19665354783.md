# Requirements - the high level principles

[View in Confluence](https://bainco.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OI30/pages/19665354783)

1. **Product strategy**

- Design architecture of OI 3.0 **to scale across all industries** from the start (testing with selected one industry, clear path to **scale across all in cost efficient way**).
- Build a common core with industry-specific adaptations where needed (e.g., FS specific vs. Manufacturing/Energy close enough for model to learn)
  - Agree quality stage gates
- Ensure natural language experience on the UI.
- Continuously improve the product based on Partner feedback.
- Add pendo (or other tracking tool) from the start.
- Allow to upload the external documents -> add a guardrails as it could be sensitive client information.

1. **Product readiness**

- Build a curated library of **50–100 certified diagnostics** to strengthen the model.
- Assess available assets from the **Bain Benchmarking CoE**.
- Prioritize real diagnostic outputs over IRIS where possible.
- Change the product name.
