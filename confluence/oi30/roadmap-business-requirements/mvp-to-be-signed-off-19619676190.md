# MVP - to be signed off

[View in Confluence](https://bainco.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OI30/pages/19619676190)

The principles:

- **Cross-sector MVP:** The MVP should support multiple industries rather than being limited to CPG. The specific sectors should be validated during Discovery, and the StatusNeo SOW should remain flexible.
- **A1 on MVP outputs (to be validated during discovery):** Key discussion points, Executive Summary, Case for Change, Size of Prize (waterfall and details), supporting appendix, reference cases, and standard Bain content. To be discussed: expert network recommendations.
  - **Quality over simplicity:** The Size of Prize should be based on triangulated data with transparent calculations and drill-down capability rather than simple benchmark comparisons.
- **Key capabilities:** The tool should provide explainability by linking outputs back to source documents and supporting user review where appropriate.
- **Key features:** output edit, tracking, upload of the documents (to be added based on discovery)
- **Data sources:** Prioritize CapIQ, LSEG, IRIS, Sage/Glynn, public sources, and user-uploaded documents, with manual uploads serving as a fallback.
- **Delivery approach:** Balance speed with scalability to be discussed based on discovery finding. Clear assessment of what feasible needed to make strategic decision on GLS presentation of Opportunity Indicator 3.0.
