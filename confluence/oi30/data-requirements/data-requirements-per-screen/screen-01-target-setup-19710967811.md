# Screen 01: Target Setup

[View in Confluence](https://bainco.atlassian.net/wiki/spaces/OI30/pages/19710967811)

Stage 1 of 5. The partner arrives here from the Dashboard by clicking 'Create new OI' or by opening an existing OI still at the Target stage. The screen has two states:

● Pre-resolution: only the target name input and Resolve button are visible.

● Post-resolution (public company): four setup cards are revealed — Context, Framing, Peer set, and a document upload nudge.

**What is actually shown on screen after resolving a public company target:**

● Target resolved state: company name, exchange ticker, sector label (e.g. Nike, Inc. · NYSE: NKE · Consumer / Retail).

● Geographic scope (optional): Global (default, pre-selected) / AMERS / EMEA / APAC / Custom (free text, e.g. Southeast Asia, DACH). Shown as chips. When non-Global selected, agent filters peer set and benchmarks to companies with significant revenue exposure in that region.

● Business unit scope (optional): All business units (default) or specific BUs — e.g. Footwear, Apparel, Equipment & other, Converse for Nike. Warning shown when BU scoping selected: limits peer benchmarking to revenue and high-level margin only — granular cost bar decomposition and SG&A are not available at product-line level.

● Account relationship card: key account partner name and avatar (e.g. Michael Chen — Key account partner, Consumer & Retail, relationship since 2019) and a list of past Bain cases on this target, each linking to Sage. Agent also mentions the key partner in the resolution chat message.

● Context (optional): who the deck is for — four audience chips (CFO conversation, CEO conversation, Board audience, Others). Free text input shown when Others is selected.

● Framing (optional): a few prompted questions so the agent steers the case the right way.

● Peer set: six comparability criteria chips — Business model, Revenue scale, Regional exposure, End market & customers, Product mix, Growth profile.

● Document upload nudge: drop files or browse. The full documents card is hidden for public companies. Confidence score card appears after documents are uploaded.
